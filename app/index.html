    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, no difficulty trophies, free roam after the credits. PowerPyx puts the Platinum at roughly 200 hours and 4/10 difficulty; the order below keeps the one possibly-missable trophy safe and lets most trophies feed each other. Every stage is built from the official trophy descriptions and the counts PlayStation tracks.

    Golden rules

    • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation at Jason's house before Chapter 1 ends — it is tied to the only possibly-missable trophy, Positive Mental Attitude.
    • No cheats on a trophy run. GTABase and TechWiser report that any cheat disables trophies and saving for the session; cheats only unlock after the story anyway.
    • Scan as you go. WAiNK vehicles, landmarks and curiosities during the story so Whip It Good and You WAiNKer! are nearly done by the credits.
    • Start stranger strands early. Some chains may have story-linked windows, and five character strands carry trophies of their own.
    • Mind the Criminal Profile. 'Irredeemable' is permanent (PowerPyx, GTABase) — relevant to Actions Speak Louder.

    Missables & warnings

    Out of 55 trophies, exactly one is reported as possibly missable. Everything else — story trophies included — can still be earned in free roam after the credits, because there is no mission replay but also nothing the ending locks away.

    Safe by design

    All 12 story trophies unlock automatically during the campaign and cannot be missed. The 100% Completion checklist (A Long Road) contains nothing missable either, per PowerPyx. The only point of no return worth respecting is the Chapter 1 meditation spot below.
